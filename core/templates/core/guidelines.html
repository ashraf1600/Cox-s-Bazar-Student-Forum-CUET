Be the senior you wanted to have

Answer questions patiently, even the ones asked many times. Disagree with ideas, not with people. No insults, harassment, hate speech or bullying, in topics, replies or private messages.

Post on the right board

Search before you ask, then pick the board that fits. A clear first line helps others find your topic later.

Blood requests

Include the blood group, hospital, date and a phone number someone will actually answer. Reply on the topic when a donor has been found, so others stop calling.

Jobs and tuition

Share real openings with enough detail to judge them: role or class, place, pay if you can, and how to apply. No pyramid schemes, paid "referrals" or anything that asks members for money up front.

Respect privacy

Phone numbers, emails and blood groups are shared here so members can help each other. Do not copy them outside the forum, add members to groups without asking, or post someone else's personal details.

Keep it honest and legal

No spam, impersonation, leaked exam papers, pirated material or political campaigning. Use your real name and your own account.

If something is wrong

Use Report in the menu on any topic or reply. Admins review reports and may remove content or deactivate accounts that break these rules. For anything urgent, email csf@cuet.ac.bd.

Your information

Your name, department, batch and work details are visible to approved members. Verified alumni also appear by name in the public alumni directory. Your address and date of birth are visible only to you and the admins. You can edit your profile at any time, and ask the committee to delete your account.
