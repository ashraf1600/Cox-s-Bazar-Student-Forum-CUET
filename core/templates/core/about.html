Our Mission

To unite students and alumni from Cox's Bazar studying at CUET, foster academic excellence, provide career support, and contribute to the development of our home district.

Our Vision

Build a strong, connected, and resourceful community of Cox's Bazar talent that uplifts each other and gives back to society.

What We Do

Academic Events

Workshops, seminars, and study circles designed to grow knowledge and skills.

Scholarships

Financial aid and support for meritorious students from our community.

Networking

Connect with alumni, professionals, and peers who can help build your future.

Why join us

Grow with like-minded leaders.

From mentorship to career guidance, our forum helps you learn, contribute, and inspire your peers.

  • Hands-on opportunities with real events and projects.
  • Peer support from an active academic community.
  • Career growth through alumni connections and workshops.

Our Story

  • 2015 - Forum established with 20 founding members.
  • 2018 - First annual reunion and cultural program.
  • 2022 - Launched scholarship program for underprivileged students.
  • 2026 - Digital platform launched to connect 500+ members.
