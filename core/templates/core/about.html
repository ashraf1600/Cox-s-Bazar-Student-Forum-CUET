Leaving Cox's Bazar for an engineering campus in Raozan is a big step. The forum makes sure nobody takes it alone: seniors help juniors with courses, halls and admissions, alumni share jobs and referrals, and everyone turns up when someone back home needs blood.

Our mission is to unite students and alumni from Cox's Bazar at CUET, support academic excellence and careers, and give back to our home district. We want a connected, resourceful community of Cox's Bazar talent that lifts each other up.
